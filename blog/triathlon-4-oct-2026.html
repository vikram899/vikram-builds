About the race

Bergman is a triathlon series organised by Deccan Sports Club, one of the best Indian triathlon organisers. It runs in different cities, and the races come in two main distances.

  • Olympic distance: 1.5 km swim, 40 km bike, 10 km run.
  • 113 (half-Ironman distance): 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike, 21.1 km run.

The series has been held in places like:

  • Pune and Ozar
  • Kolhapur
  • Mysore
  • Bangalore
  • Kolkata

This edition was in Ozar, near Pune, on 4 October 2026, and I did the Olympic distance, my first triathlon at that distance.

The course in a few lines: where the swim was, how many bike loops, how hilly, what the run looked like, the cut-off times.

Why this race and not Ironman Goa?

Since this was my first triathlon, I did not want to start with a full half-Ironman like Ironman Goa. I wanted the Olympic distance, and I wanted a race that was a little cheaper and close to where I live.

Bergman Ozar ticked the boxes that mattered to me:

  • The distance: Olympic, a sensible first step.
  • The cost: a little cheaper than Ironman Goa.
  • Close to home: less travelling and easier logistics for me and the bike.
  • The organisers: Deccan Sports Club has been running triathlons for some time, which matters a lot for a first triathlon.

Weighing all of that together, cost, transport, logistics and the quality of the organisation, this was the right race for me.
