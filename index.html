CHECKPOINT04THE SESSIONS

What a training week actually looks like.

Not a plan pinned to a fridge — the rotation that actually happens, most weeks.

Cardio

Everything on legs and lungs — split easy / tempo / long / interval.

Easy conversational pace, no watch-checking, no ego
  • Pace: conversational, zone 2 heart rate
  • Duration: 40–60 minutes
  • Terrain: flat road or easy trail
  • Purpose: aerobic base + recovery
  • Effort: could hold a conversation the whole way
Tempo comfortably uncomfortable — the one that actually hurts a little
  • Pace: comfortably hard, threshold effort
  • Structure: 2mi warm-up, 20–30min @ threshold, 1mi cool-down
  • Terrain: flat, consistent surface
  • Purpose: raises the lactate threshold
  • Effort: short sentences only
Long the weekend one that builds the base everything else stands on
  • Pace: easy-to-moderate, negative split on good days
  • Distance: 18–22km
  • Terrain: mixed — road, trail, whatever's around
  • Purpose: builds the aerobic base
  • Effort: starts easy, finishes earned
Interval short, hard, repeatable — hills most weeks, track when there's access
  • Pace: hard effort, short hard reps
  • Structure: 6x400m (or hill repeats) @ hard, equal recovery jog
  • Terrain: hills, or a track when there's one nearby
  • Purpose: top-end speed + leg strength
  • Effort: makes the next rep a negotiation
3x / week

Strength Training

The unglamorous work that keeps the rest of it possible — split push / pull / legs.

Push chest, shoulders, triceps — bench, overhead press, dips
  • Bench press — 4x8
  • Overhead press — 3x10
  • Incline dumbbell press — 3x10
  • Triceps dips — 3x max reps
  • Lateral raises — 3x12
Pull back, biceps, rear delts — rows, pull-ups, deadlift variations
  • Deadlifts — 4x6
  • Barbell rows — 4x8
  • Pull-ups — 3x max reps
  • Face pulls — 3x15
  • Bicep curls — 3x12
Legs quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves — the ones that make stairs a problem the next day
  • Back squats — 4x8
  • Romanian deadlifts — 3x10
  • Walking lunges — 3x12/side
  • Leg press — 3x10
  • Calf raises — 4x15

CHECKPOINT07FIELD NOTES

Why the DNFs get posted too.

entry — mile 41, somewhere past the last aid station

Legs stopped negotiating around hour six. The watch died, the headlamp battery followed it, and the only thing still running was stubbornness. That's the part that doesn't make the reel — not the finish chute, the part where you're doing math on how many miles are left in the dark and deciding it doesn't matter.

Everything on this page comes out of logs like that one. Not polished recaps — actual field notes, written slower than they happened, about what fitness and endurance sport keep teaching me about showing up anyway.
